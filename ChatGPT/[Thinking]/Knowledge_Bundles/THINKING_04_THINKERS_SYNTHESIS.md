- use: decisions overloaded by alternatives, unclear premises, limited attention, or a disputed problem representation;
- workflow: define mandatory constraints and aspiration thresholds, separate facts from values and guesses, test the problem representation, search a bounded option set, choose a reversible sufficient action, and reopen when feedback violates a threshold;
- boundary: satisficing cannot lower safety, legal, ethical, rights, evidence, or irreversible-downside constraints;
- evidence: complete P0/P1 corpus, four traceable excerpts, Judge pass;

## Conflict Map
- Central redesign vs polycentric fit: change evidenced constraints while preserving bounded local autonomy and conflict paths.
- Act under friction vs model first: match modeling depth to reversibility, impact, and feedback risk.
- System cause vs direct accountability: separate causal classification from accountability design and immediate containment.
- Deliberate review vs decision friction: trigger framing review only above a declared impact, irreversibility, or sensitivity threshold.
Each conflict requires Position A, Position B, synthesis, when each dominates, failure mode, evidence status, and source artifact IDs in the source registry.

## CONFLICT-001-CENTRAL-REDESIGN-VS-POLYCENTRIC-FIT
- positions: diagnose apparatus/concentrated dependencies before redesign vs preserve bounded decision centers and contextual local fit.
- synthesis: change only evidenced structural constraints while preserving bounded local autonomy, monitoring, and conflict paths.
- first dominates: a specific rule, permission, or concentrated dependency demonstrably reproduces failure.
- second dominates: local knowledge, shared resources, and multiple legitimate owners require differentiated rules.
- failure mode: central redesign erases local fit, or polycentric language protects a harmful bottleneck.
- evidence status: source-backed author patterns; cross-author resolution provisional.
- source artifacts: `THINKERS-LENIN-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-LENIN-JUDGE-001`, `THINKERS-OSTROM-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-OSTROM-JUDGE-001`.
## CONFLICT-002-ACT-UNDER-FRICTION-VS-MODEL-FIRST
- positions: take bounded action after dependencies are explicit vs model before live intervention when feedback/delay matters.
- synthesis: match modeling depth to reversibility, impact, and feedback risk; use deterministic modeling for high-impact delayed intervention.
- first dominates: action is reversible, time-sensitive, and additional model inputs add little decision value.
- second dominates: effects are delayed, nonlinear, system-wide, or expensive to reverse.
- failure mode: analysis paralysis on routine work, or intervention from an implicit linear model.
- evidence status: source-backed author patterns; cross-author resolution provisional.
- source artifacts: `THINKERS-CLAUSEWITZ-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-CLAUSEWITZ-JUDGE-001`, `THINKERS-FORRESTER-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-FORRESTER-JUDGE-001`.
## CONFLICT-003-SYSTEM-CAUSE-VS-DIRECT-ACCOUNTABILITY
- positions: classify system/attributable/mixed/unknown cause vs assign mandates, monitoring, conflict resolution, and rollback ownership.
- synthesis: keep causal classification separate from accountability design; contain direct violations, then assign correction to the level able to change the cause.
- first dominates: repeated outcomes still lack a supported cause class.
- second dominates: cause is understood but mandates, monitoring, or conflict paths are unclear.
- failure mode: system explanation erases accountability, or governance assigns blame without causal evidence.
- evidence status: source-backed author patterns; cross-author resolution provisional.
- source artifacts: `THINKERS-DEMING-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-DEMING-JUDGE-001`, `THINKERS-OSTROM-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-OSTROM-JUDGE-001`.
## CONFLICT-004-DELIBERATE-REVIEW-VS-DECISION-FRICTION
- positions: record baseline/frames/initial judgment before a consequential decision vs avoid uncertainty review as generic delay.
- synthesis: trigger framing review only when impact, irreversibility, or plausible frame sensitivity crosses a declared threshold.
- first dominates: equivalent framing could materially change a consequential judgment.
- second dominates: the task is routine, reversible, and review costs exceed evidence value.
- failure mode: bias review becomes hindsight labeling, or friction review skips material evidence.
- evidence status: source-backed author patterns; cross-author resolution provisional.
- source artifacts: `THINKERS-KAHNEMAN-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-KAHNEMAN-JUDGE-001`, `THINKERS-CLAUSEWITZ-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-CLAUSEWITZ-JUDGE-001`.

### `SYN-001-REPEATED-FAILURE-DIAGNOSIS`
**Repeated Failure Diagnosis**
- problem type: `repeated_process_failure`
- source authors: Deming, Forrester, Clausewitz
- source patterns: `THINKERS-DEMING-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-FORRESTER-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-CLAUSEWITZ-PATTERN-001`
- use: recurring failure persists after local fixes or may involve system feedback, delay, or unresolved dependencies.
- do not use: one-off direct violation is evidenced or urgent containment cannot wait.
- workflow: separate signal/requirement; map recurrence, scope, feedback, delay, and dependencies; classify cause; assign bounded action; define verification and rollback.
- output: bounded diagnosis with cause class, loop/dependency hypotheses, owners, verification trigger, and rollback.
- risks: unsupported statistical labels; accountability dilution; qualitative loops presented as proof.
- QA: facts, requirements, hypotheses, feedback assumptions, owners, and overturning evidence are distinguishable.
- rollback: revert the corrective change if verification contradicts the diagnosis; retain and reclassify the incident.

### `SYN-002-DECISION-UNDER-FRICTION`
**Decision Under Friction**
- problem type: `consequential_decision_under_uncertainty`
- source authors: Clausewitz, Kahneman, Forrester
- source patterns: `THINKERS-CLAUSEWITZ-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-KAHNEMAN-PATTERN-001`, `THINKERS-FORRESTER-PATTERN-001`
- use: consequential decision combines uncertainty, material dependencies, frame sensitivity, or delayed system effects.
- do not use: routine reversible work with no material dependency, framing, or feedback risk.
- workflow: state objective/dependencies; record baseline and frames; map evidenced feedback; choose review depth/action; declare monitoring and rollback.
- output: decision record with evidence, frame test, dependencies, model requirement, action, and revisit trigger.
- risks: militarized framing; bias labels without evidence; false model precision.

- do not use: retrospective diagnosis of a person, substitution for domain evidence, or delay on routine reversible work.

- risks: hindsight accusation; post-hoc baseline; slow review assumed superior.
